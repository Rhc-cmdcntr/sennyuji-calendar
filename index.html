Reading the projections

  • Confirmed items are fixed annual dates from an official temple page, or already announced for this year. Projected items are placed from 2025 patterns and firm up once the year's dates post.
  • October carries two BLOCK days a week apart: 即成院 お練り供養 (3rd Sunday, a ~300-person bodhisattva procession) and OTOBUTAI (an evening production that takes over the head-temple grounds).
  • 泉山七福神巡り (Coming-of-Age Day, the 2nd Monday of January) is the busiest winter day, spanning nine sub-temples.
  • Autumn foliage and the 雲龍院 evening light-up (~mid-to-late November) are projected from 2025; re-check the exact dates in late October.
  • 泉山涅槃会・大涅槃図公開 (Mar 14-16) is the largest spring event; the roughly 16m painting is shown from around Mar 8.
